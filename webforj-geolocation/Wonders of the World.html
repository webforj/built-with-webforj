Cartographer's Note

Feign Your Position

Wish to measure from Cairo, the South Pole, or your childhood home? Your browser can pretend to stand anywhere on Earth.

❧
  1. Open the Developer Tools

    Press F12, or Ctrl+Shift+I (⌘+⌥+I on macOS).

  2. Summon the Sensors panel

    Open the Command Menu with Ctrl/⌘+Shift+P, type “Sensors,” and choose Show Sensors.

  3. Set a custom location

    Under Location, pick a preset city or choose Other… and type any latitude and longitude.

  4. Watch the codex recompute

    Every distance and bearing on this page updates the moment your phantom position changes.

Firefox & Edge offer the same panel; in Safari, use the custom-coordinates field above instead.
